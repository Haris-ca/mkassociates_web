Company Incorporation

Choose the Right
Business Structure

We help you set up the right legal entity — from sole proprietorships to private limited companies — with complete registration support.

👤

Proprietary Concern

No legal distinction between the business and the owner. The owner assumes full responsibility for the business's operations, liabilities, and profits.

  • Ownership
  • Control
  • Decision-Making
🤝

Partnership Firms

Two or more individuals come together to carry on a business with the shared goal of making a profit.

  • Agreement
  • Flexibility
  • Shared Profits
🏢

LLP Firms

Business structure combining features of both a partnership and a corporation, providing limited liability protection to its partners.

  • Flexible Ownership
  • Pass-through Taxation
  • Professional Autonomy
🏛️

Private Limited Co.

Business structure offering limited liability protection to shareholders, with a separate legal entity distinct from its owners.

  • Limited Liability
  • Separate Legal Entity
  • Management Structure
🏛️

Trust / Section 8 Co.

Individuals formally establish a society or trust as a legal entity, fulfilling legal requirements as outlined by relevant laws.

  • Tax Benefits
  • Recognition & Legitimacy
  • Governance & Transparency
